Tuna Pie

Looking for an easy and affordable homemade snack that everyone in the family will enjoy? Crispy Tuna Pies are made with simple pantry ingredients and are quick to prepare. They're perfect as a tasty midday snack, appetizer, or light meal.

Ingredients ⋆‧°𓏲ּ𝄢

  • Sliced white bread
  • Canned tuna
  • Red onions
  • Mayonnaise
  • Cheese
  • Milk and egg
  • Bread crumbs
  • Oil

Steps >⩊<.ᐟ

  1. Combine tuna, mayonnaise, onions, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Cut quick-melt cheese into strips and set aside.
    2.  
  2. Trim the edges of the sliced bread and flatten until very thin using a rolling pin.
    3.  
  3. Spoon tuna filling on the lower part of the bread and place a cheese strip on top.
    4.  
  4. Fold the upper half of the bread over the filling. Using the tines of a fork, press on the edges to fully seal.
    5.  
  5. Whisk eggs and milk in a bowl until well blended. Briefly dip the bread pocket in egg mixture and roll in crumbs, patting down to fully coat.
    6.  
  6. Carefully add the breaded pies in a single layer to about two inches of hot oil and deep-fry until crunchy and golden. Remove from the pan and drain on a wire rack.

Additional Tip/Reminders °❀⋆

  • Drain the canned tuna well to prevent an overly wet filling from tearing the bread. Stir together the salad ingredients when ready to assemble to keep the filling from getting too watery.
    •  
  • Dip the pies in the milk/egg mixture very briefly lest the moisture will make the bread soggy.
